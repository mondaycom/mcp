# daily-briefing — brief templates

Loaded by `SKILL.md` Step 7. Section names here match the synthesis buckets in Step 6 one-to-one. Omit any section with no items. Header counts must equal the number of lines printed under them; in `quick`, write `(<total>, top 2)`.

When posting with `create_update`, convert to HTML (`<h3>`, `<b>`, `<ul><li>`) and send it as one line with no newlines between tags — `create_update` does not render Markdown, and monday turns newlines into extra `<br>` blank lines. Keep the footer as visible text; HTML comments are stripped. `create_doc` and `update_doc` take the Markdown as is.

## Pipeline

```markdown
# CRM Morning Briefing — <weekday>, <Mon DD>

## Pipeline at a glance
- Active pipeline: $<total> across <N> deals · weighted $<forecast>
- Closing this week: <N> deals, $<value>
- <activity line, e.g. "7 activities logged in the last 24h — 3 calls, 2 emails, 1 meeting, 1 note">

## Yesterday's movements
- Won: <deal> ($<value>, <owner>)
- Lost: <deal> ($<value>, <owner>)

## Tasks due today (<N>)
- <task or deal> — <what>, owner <name>

## Needs you today

### Closing today (<N>)
- <deal> — <stage>, $<value>, owner <name>, last touch <X days ago>

### Slipping (<N>)
- <deal> — <stage>, $<value>, expected <date> (<X> days overdue), owner <name>

### Going cold (<N>)
- <deal> — <stage>, no touch in <X> days, owner <name>

### Follow-up (<N>)
- <deal> — <stage>, <probability>%, last touch <X days ago>

## Recent meetings (<N>)
- <meeting title> → <deal> — <one-line takeaway>
- <meeting title> — not linked to a deal

## On track (<N>)
- <deal> — <stage>, <probability>%, closing in <X> days

## New this week (<N>)
- <deal> — <stage>, added <date>

## Data hygiene
- <N> deals missing owner · <N> missing close date · <N> missing amount

---
Generated by Claude · daily-briefing · <YYYY-MM-DD> · run /monday-crm:daily-briefing to refresh.
```

Lower-priority signals on a deal shown in a higher bucket are annotated in text, e.g. `— also going cold (19 days)`.
